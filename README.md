Document version: 1.5.0 (effective October 24, 2026).

India and Indonesia are marked as launch preparation. Existing distribution
regions are South Korea, the United States, Japan and Brazil.

This revision adds Hindi and Indonesian policy pages, regional notices in all
six languages, phased commencement of India's DPDP framework, and Indonesian
rights, cross-border-transfer and incident-notification information.

Publication does not implement a consent mechanism or establish compliance with
transfer requirements. Verify Google contracts, SDK/UMP settings and the shipped
build. UMP is not described as automatically obtaining all consents required in
India or Indonesia. PSE applicability and registration are a separate launch
matter; this policy makes no claim that registration is complete or unnecessary.

The application language links must be shipped in an app update. Play Console
localized privacy-policy links are managed separately from this repository.
