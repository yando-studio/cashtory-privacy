Y&O STUDIO (the “Developer”) respects the privacy and rights of CashTory users. This Privacy Policy explains what information CashTory processes, where it is stored, and how users can manage it.

At a glance: CashTory has no account, login, or Developer-operated server. Budget records and transaction data saved from receipts remain on the user’s device, and the Developer cannot access them. The Google advertising SDK and Google ML Kit may transmit the technical information described below to Google. If a user chooses to email the Developer through the Contact feature, the message and certain app and device information may be sent to the Developer’s email account.

1. Information processed and where it is stored

1.1 Information stored only on the user’s device

CashTory processes information entered or saved by the user in a local SQLite database, including:

  • income, expense, and transfer records;
  • asset, liability, card, and installment information;
  • categories, recurring transactions, favorites, and notes;
  • settings such as theme, language, currency, and month start date; and
  • merchant name, date, total, and transaction details the user chooses to save from a receipt.

The Developer does not operate a server for this information and cannot remotely access or view it.

1.2 Technical information Google SDKs may process automatically

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK: IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app and ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, Android advertising ID, and app set ID.
  • Google ML Kit: device and app information, device or installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input and output size, feature version, model-download and feature events, resource-release events, and error codes.

Receipt images and recognized text are processed on the device and are not sent to the Developer’s servers or Google’s servers. Sections 6, 13, and 14 provide more information about SDK processing.

1.3 Information sent through Contact

When a user selects Contact, the device’s email app opens. The email body may be prefilled with the app version, operating system name and version, device model, app language, and currency setting. The user can review or remove this information before sending the email.

Only after the user sends the email will the Developer receive the sender’s email address, message, and any included app and device information. The Developer uses this information only to respond, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

1.4 Information the Developer does not ordinarily collect

CashTory has no account or login. During ordinary use, the Developer does not separately collect names, phone numbers, contacts, call logs, precise GPS location, government identification numbers, passport numbers, or legally protected sensitive information. Firebase Analytics is not separately integrated. Information voluntarily included in an email is handled as described above.

3. Camera, photos, and receipt recognition

3.1 Google ML Kit Document Scanner

On supported Android devices, Google ML Kit Document Scanner is the default capture method. Google Play services provides the capture screen, ML models, and related resources for detecting receipt edges and correcting perspective. Available features may vary by Google Play services version.

Google Play services uses its own camera permission and sends CashTory only the result image selected by the user. If required resources are not already installed, Google Play services may download them.

3.2 CashTory camera and Android photo picker

CashTory requests camera permission only when the document scanner is unavailable or fails and the app switches to its own camera. The Android system photo picker provides only the image selected by the user without granting access to the entire photo library.

3.3 On-device text recognition

CashTory uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition to recognize receipt text on the device. Supported scripts include Latin, Korean, Japanese, and Devanagari. A required model may be downloaded through Google Play services during installation or first use.

  • Receipt images and recognized text are not sent to the Developer’s servers or Google’s servers.
  • Receipt images and full recognized text are not stored in the app database.
  • A scanner result may temporarily remain in the device’s temporary storage and is not separately deleted by CashTory.
  • CashTory does not delete an original image selected from the user’s photo library.
  • Only the merchant, date, total, and transactions the user chooses to save remain in the local database.

ML Kit may send the technical information described in Section 1.2 to Google for diagnostics and usage measurement. That information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

4. Advertising

The Android version of CashTory uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display ads. For ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, the SDK may collect or share IP address, approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, Android advertising ID, and app set ID.

Where consent or privacy choices are required, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) may display an advertising privacy message. Users in eligible regions can revisit available choices through More → Ad privacy settings. In applicable U.S. states, the message may include choices relating to personalized or targeted advertising and the sale or sharing of personal information, subject to applicable law and Google’s available controls.

Declining personalized advertising does not affect CashTory’s core budgeting features, although the type of ad may change. Users may also manage the advertising ID and Google ad settings through Android settings and Google’s My Ad Center.

Advertising is included only in the currently distributed Android version. If an iOS version is released or its advertising practices change, this Policy will be updated to reflect the actual implementation.

5. Storage and backups

5.1 Local storage

Budget data is stored in CashTory’s app-specific SQLite database on the user’s device. It is not stored on a Developer-operated server or cloud service.

5.2 In-app automatic backups

CashTory may create automatic backup files in app-specific storage so the user can restore an earlier state. When the configured limit is exceeded, older backups are deleted first. These backups remain on the device.

5.3 Manual backup and export

CashTory exports JSON backups or CSV files only when the user initiates the action. Android may display its system file or folder picker so the user can choose the destination. CashTory does not gain broad access to device storage.

If a user sends an exported file through email, messaging, or cloud storage, that service’s privacy and retention terms apply. Exported files are not automatically deleted when CashTory is uninstalled.

5.4 Android backup and device transfer

CashTory sets allowBackup=false and does not use Android automatic cloud backup. On some devices running Android 12 or later, this setting may block cloud backup but may not prevent direct device-to-device transfer by the device manufacturer.

Users should create a manual JSON backup before uninstalling CashTory or changing devices.

8. Children’s privacy

CashTory’s target age setting on Google Play is 18 and over. CashTory is not directed to children under 14 in South Korea or children under 13 in the United States. It has no account or age-verification feature, and the Developer does not knowingly collect names, contact details, or other personal information from children in those age groups through the app.

If the Developer learns that a child below the applicable age sent personal information through email or another channel, the Developer will take reasonable steps required by law to verify and delete it. A parent or guardian may contact the Developer using the details in Section 12.

9. User rights and choices

  • Users can view, correct, and delete budget records directly in CashTory.
  • Users can obtain their data through JSON backup or CSV export.
  • Camera permission can be granted or withdrawn through Android settings.
  • The Android photo picker does not require access to the entire photo library.
  • Users can reset or delete the advertising ID and manage Google ad settings through Android settings.
  • Where available, advertising choices can be changed through More → Ad privacy settings.

To ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent regarding information held by the Developer, contact the email address in Section 12. The Developer may request the minimum information needed to verify the requester or an authorized representative.

Because the Developer does not hold local budget records, the Developer cannot access, correct, or delete them on the user’s behalf. Rights regarding information independently processed by Google may be exercised as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

10. Security

  • Budget records remain on the user’s device and are not sent to a Developer-operated server.
  • Receipt images and full recognized text are not stored in the app database.
  • The release build does not request microphone, broad photo/media, or broad external-storage permissions.
  • Technical information sent by Google Mobile Ads SDK and ML Kit is encrypted in transit using TLS or HTTPS.

No storage method is completely secure. Users should use a device screen lock, keep Android updated, and store exported JSON and CSV files securely.

Advertising choices should be managed through the in-app privacy setting and Google or Android ad settings. Where applicable law provides these rights, users may ask the Developer to confirm the categories and purposes of personal information it holds, provide access, correct or delete information, or opt out of certain processing. Send requests to the email address in Section 12 with the subject “U.S. Privacy Request.” The Developer will request only the information reasonably necessary to verify the request and will respond within the time and subject to the exceptions provided by applicable law.

An authorized agent may submit a request where permitted. The Developer may verify the user’s identity and the agent’s authority. If a request is denied, the user may appeal through the same email address where applicable law provides an appeal right. The Developer will not unlawfully discriminate against a user for exercising privacy rights.
